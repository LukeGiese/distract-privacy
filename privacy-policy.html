Distract privacy policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026
Publisher: Luke Giese
Contact: Lukealexgiese@gmail.com

Distract helps you focus through study sessions and blocking selected distracting websites. It works as a standalone browser extension without an account, developer server or desktop companion.

Information processed and stored

Distract reads tab addresses and responds to top-level browser navigation events to decide whether the active page in the focused browser window is on your distraction list or is an exact allowed page. This is processing of browsing activity, even though it happens locally. Distract does not read webpage contents, cookies, passwords, microphone input or the browser history database.

Local extension storage holds your settings, domain and path rules, exact allowed URLs and their labels, topics, goals, study descriptions and notes, manual entries, timestamped study and break intervals, and active-session checkpoints. Avoid putting sensitive information in labels or notes if you do not want it in these records.

For currently blocked tabs, Distract temporarily saves the original full URL, a random restoration token, tab identifier and creation time. It uses these to restore the page when blocking ends or the address becomes allowed. These URLs can include query parameters and fragments. They are not added to study history or exported backups. Restoration entries are removed after restoration or when a tab is no longer relevant. After a browser restart, entries for tabs that have not yet restored may remain for up to 24 hours and are pruned the next time the extension runs.

Session-only browser storage holds flags and a random navigation identifier used to distinguish browser restarts from service-worker suspension. It does not contain a general browsing log.

Where information goes

Distract uses local browser-profile storage, not browser sync. It does not send your records or browsing addresses to the publisher or a third-party service. There are no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, accounts or remote classification services. Executable code, lists, icons and sound are packaged with the extension.

The websites you choose to open or that are restored in a tab receive normal browser requests and are governed by their own policies. Blocking happens after navigation; it does not prevent all network requests to a listed site. Chrome Web Store installation/update services and your browser or operating system may have their own data practices independent of Distract.

Settings includes an optional donation link to https://ko-fi.com/distractextension. Clicking it opens the external Ko-fi website in a new tab. The extension does not send your study records, settings or browsing addresses with this link, embed payment forms, or process payment information. Information you choose to provide on the external site is governed by that site’s policies.

Optional completion notifications may display a session description through the browser and operating system. Sound and keep-screen-awake controls run on your device. No information is sold, used for advertising, transferred to data brokers or used for credit decisions.

Retention and deletion

Settings and saved study records remain until you change, replace or delete them, or uninstall the extension. You can delete individual history entries and website rules or allowed pages in the interface. Archiving a topic keeps its history; it is not deletion. Restore defaults replaces website/path rules only; it does not erase study records or allowed pages. Importing a backup replaces settings, lists, topics, goals and history after validation. Uninstalling normally removes browser-managed extension storage; remove any retained profile copies or device backups separately.

Your backup files

Export is optional and creates a JSON file containing your settings, rules, exact allowed URLs, topics, goals and session records, including descriptions and notes. An active session is included at its checkpoint. Temporary tab restoration URLs and unknown legacy configuration fields are excluded. Importing your chosen file is local; it does not upload the file to a server. Keep exports private and delete them yourself when no longer needed. Uninstalling does not delete downloaded files or copies you made elsewhere.

Questions and changes

Contact lukealexgiese@gmail.com with privacy questions. The publisher cannot retrieve or delete your local records remotely. We will update this policy if the extension's data practices change.

Distract's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
